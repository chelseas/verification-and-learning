Explore bound propagation.

Bounds are often propagated layer by layer through a network. The tightness of the bounds depends on the propagation method. For example, Interval Bound Propagation (IBP) is fast but loose, while other methods liked Mixed Integer Programming (MIP) are slower but tighter.
